# Discovery Survey Questions

## Theme 1: Project Goals & Vision
1. In one sentence, what is the primary problem this app/website is solving?
2. What are the top 3 goals you want to achieve in the first 6 months of launch?
3. How will you measure success (e.g., number of signups, revenue, user retention)?

## Theme 2: Target Audience & Personas
4. Describe your ideal user. Who are they, and what are their biggest pain points?
5. What is the primary "job" the user wants to accomplish when they open your app?

## Theme 3: Core Features & User Journeys
6. Walk me through the "Happy Path": What are the steps a user takes from first opening the app to completing their main goal?
7. Are there any "must-have" features for the MVP (Minimum Viable Product)?
8. Are there any "nice-to-have" features that we should plan for future phases?

## Theme 4: Data & Information Flow
9. What specific information do you need to collect from your users (e.g., name, email, preferences, photos)?
10. How do you currently manage this information (e.g., spreadsheets, paper, an old system)?
11. Is there any specific data that needs to be handled with extra care (e.g., health data, financial info)?

## Theme 5: Business Processes & Workflows
12. Describe the internal business process this app supports. How does it work today?
13. What are the manual steps in your current process that you want to automate?

## Theme 6: Compliance & Security
14. Besides GDPR, are there any other specific regulatory requirements for your industry?
15. Are there any specific security concerns you have (e.g., "I'm worried about unauthorized access to X")?
